## Thin Line Role 🎖️

A cosmetic role for active or former **Firefighters, EMTs, Paramedics, Military (Foreign & Domestic), and Law Enforcement**.

**How to get it:** Run `/verify` in any channel. The bot will DM you to confirm your field of work and optionally accept supporting documents (with PII redacted).

This is an honor system — misuse will result in **two warns up to a ban**.
