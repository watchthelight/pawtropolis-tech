# #00258 -- Verification identity documents are retained indefinitely in the log channel with no deletion policy

**Type:** security | **Priority:** Low

The verification flow (src/commands/verify.ts) posts user-uploaded identity documents (badges/IDs) as embed images into the verification log channel so staff can review them. After #00162 the channel is configurable via VERIFY_LOG_CHANNEL_ID, but the underlying privacy concern remains: the images are durable PII stored in a Discord channel indefinitely, the attachment CDN URLs stay accessible, and there is no retention or deletion policy. This is honor-system redaction, so un-redacted government-issued IDs may be retained.

**Location:** `src/commands/verify.ts` (document posting + retention); verification log channel
**Category:** security / data-retention (product decision)

**Suggested fix (needs a product decision):** Decide and document a retention policy for verification documents. Options: (a) auto-delete the document log message after the application is decided or after a fixed TTL; (b) store only a boolean "documents provided" plus a short-lived reference instead of durable images; (c) restrict the log channel to least-privilege staff and document a manual purge cadence. This changes the staff verification workflow, so confirm intended behavior before implementing.

**Evidence:** out-of-scope portion of #00162 (configurable-channel fix landed; retention redesign deferred as a deliberate product decision).
