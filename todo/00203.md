# #00203 -- Modmail close transcript buffer leaks in memory when no log channel is configured

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** Low

closeModmailThread relies entirely on flushTranscript to clear the per-ticket in-memory transcript buffer. flushTranscript only deletes transcriptBuffers entries on its success paths (transcript.ts:203 and 262). When `modmail_log_channel_id` is not configured it returns early at transcript.ts:119-122 WITHOUT deleting the buffer, and the exception path (transcript.ts:265-283) also never deletes it. closeModmailThread never calls the exported clearTranscriptBuffer as a safety net (grep shows it is only re-exported, never invoked in the close flow). Result: on every modmail close in a guild with no log channel set, the ticket's transcript lines remain in the module-level transcriptBuffers Map forever, growing unbounded with message volume. Same gap applies to closeModmailForApplication (lines 505-511).

**Location:** `src/features/modmail/threadClose.ts:294-307`
**Category:** leak | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** After flushTranscript returns (regardless of outcome), call clearTranscriptBuffer(ticketId) in both closeModmailThread and closeModmailForApplication; or have flushTranscript delete the buffer in a finally block / on the no-log-channel early return.

**Evidence:** full-codebase multi-agent audit 2026-05-31, finding A0095. See audit/2026-05-31/full-codebase-audit.md.
