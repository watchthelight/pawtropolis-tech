# #00168 -- Heatmap can include messages outside the requested range when span isn't a multiple of 7 days

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** Low

getHeatmapDataForRange computes weeksCount = ceil(spanS / 7d) and then carves 7-day windows strictly backwards from endS: weekEndS = endS - offset*7d, weekStartS = weekEndS - 7d. The oldest window therefore starts at endS - weeksCount*7d, which is EARLIER than the requested range.startS whenever (endS - startS) is not an exact multiple of 7 days. For a custom window (e.g., a 10-day or 40-day range from the dashboard time picker), this pulls message_activity rows from before the user-selected start into the totals/trends, so totalMessages, avgMessagesPerHour, busiest/least-active hours and week-over-week growth are computed over a wider span than requested. The COUNT/trends silently over-report.

**Location:** `web/src/lib/server/queries/heatmap.ts:174-184`
**Category:** correctness | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Clamp the oldest window to startS: compute weekStartS = Math.max(weekStartS, startS) for the final iteration (or derive weeksCount so the earliest weekStartS == startS). Alternatively iterate forward from startS and let the last week be the partial one, clamping weekEndS to endS.
