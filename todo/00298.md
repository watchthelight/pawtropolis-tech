# #00298 -- Dashboard leftovers found while the Quality tab was rebuilt

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** Low

Three things noticed in the dashboard on 2026-11-15 during #00295. None of them was changed there.

- **Four pages set the cache header before they refuse.** The loaders of the Heatmap, Pulse, Stats and Audit pages call `setHeaders` with `private, max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=300` and check the tier after that. If SvelteKit sends that header with the error page, a browser keeps the refusal for up to a minute for someone who was given the role in the meantime. This was read in the code and not checked against a real response. The Quality page checks first and sets the header second (`web/src/routes/dashboard/quality/+page.server.ts`), and `tests/web/pages/quality.test.ts` holds that order. The four others can follow it.
- **The label of a custom time window has an en dash.** `formatWindowLabel` in `web/src/lib/shared/timeWindow.ts` puts one between the two dates. The repo's rule is no em dash and no en dash in user-facing text.
- **Dashes in the dashboard's source.** 72 files under `web/src` hold 195 em or en dashes, almost all of them in comments from before v26.
