# #00225 -- Helper tests assert against mocks/inline literals and never invoke the functions under test

**Type:** refactor | **Priority:** Low

The `describe`s for `isStaff`, `requireInteractionStaff`, `resolveApplication`, and all the `openRejectModal`/`openAcceptModal`/`openPermRejectModal`/`openKickModal`/`openUnclaimModal` blocks do not import or call any of those real functions (defined in src/features/review/handlers/helpers.ts). Instead they call the mocked dependencies directly (e.g. `hasRole(...)`, `findAppByShortCode(...)`, `getClaim(...)`) or assert on inline-recomputed expressions like `["rejected","approved","kicked"].includes(app.status)` and `\`v1:modal:reject:code${code}\``. These are tautologies that re-test JavaScript/Vitest, not the module. The real `openRejectModal` has non-trivial ordering (status check, claimGuard, then `safeShowModal` token-age/replied checks at helpers.ts:138-202) that is entirely unverified. Concrete impact: the suite reports green while providing no protection against regressions in staff gating or modal pre-checks.

**Location:** `tests/features/review/handlers/helpers.test.ts:56-188, 294-462`
**Category:** dead-code | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Import the real functions and exercise them with mocked discord.js interaction objects, asserting on observable effects (replyOrEdit content, whether showModal is/ isn't called, return value of requireInteractionStaff). Remove the tautological assertions or convert them into behavioral tests.

**Evidence:** full-codebase multi-agent audit 2026-05-31, finding A0197. See audit/2026-05-31/full-codebase-audit.md.
