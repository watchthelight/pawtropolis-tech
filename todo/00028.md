# #00028 -- Move deploy lock out of /tmp

**Type:** chore | **Priority:** Medium

deploy.sh:47 sets DEPLOY_LOCK_DIR="/tmp/pawtropolis-deploy.lock". /tmp is world-writable on Unix; any user on the EC2 box could rm -rf the lock and hijack a concurrent deploy.

Action:
1. Move lock to `${REMOTE_PATH}/.deploy.lock` (inside the app dir with restricted perms) or `/home/ubuntu/pawtropolis-deploy.lock`.
2. Update mkdir / rm logic in deploy.sh.
3. Verify the lock works under contention (run two deploys in parallel; second should refuse).
