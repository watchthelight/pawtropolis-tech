# #00297 -- Quality tab: answer the all-time window from weekly sums

**Type:** refactor | **Priority:** Low

The Quality tab of the dashboard (#00295) reads its window in two passes over the score file: one grouped by week and effort bin, one grouped by author. For the default window of 30 days that costs nothing worth naming. For the window "all" both passes read every score, 1,965,039 of them on 2026-11-15.

Measured on 2026-11-15 on the score file of the whole history:

- On the host: 2.4 s for the pass by week and 1.7 to 1.8 s for the pass by author, about 4.1 s together. The last 30 days take 65 ms (52 and 13).
- On the host again, on the live file after the deploy of 26.2.0, ten times in half an hour: 2.3 to 3.1 s and 1.7 to 2.3 s, 4.0 to 5.2 s together. The last 30 days took 66 to 82 ms.
- On the dev machine: 1.4 to 1.6 s and 1.3 to 1.5 s for "all", 76 ms for 90 days, 33 ms for 30 days and 17 ms for 7 days.

The dashboard runs its queries on its one thread, so every other page waits for those four to five seconds. The page keeps the answer for a window for half an hour (`CACHE_TTL.veryLong`), so it happens once per half hour at most, and only when someone picks "all" or a long custom window. The file grows by about 1,000 scores a day.

**What would fix it.** The research job already keeps one row per week in `weekly_overlay`. It could keep two more small tables in the score file: per week and effort bin (the count and the sums of effort, resonance and substantiveness), and per week and author (the count and the sum of effort). The page would add up whole weeks from those tables and read single scores only for the days at the two edges of a window. The pass by week over the whole history gave 3,708 groups on 2026-11-15, so the all-time window would read a few thousand rows in place of two million. The table per week and author is the larger of the two, and its size has to be measured before it is built.

**What to check first.** Whether anyone opens the all-time window often enough for this to matter. The dashboard's access log has the address with `window=all`.

**Not a fix.** An index does not help, because both passes need every row of the window. A longer cache time only moves the wait.
