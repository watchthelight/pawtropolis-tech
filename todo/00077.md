# #00077 -- Patreon art entitlements with quantity > 1 are permanently under-granted - the second emoji ticket is lost on 

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** High

PATREON_ART_ENTITLEMENTS gives '[Patreon] Legendary Fiona' emoji:2 (line 39 region). handlePatreonArtRewards computes delta = entitlement - alreadyGranted, assigns the ticket ROLE exactly once regardless of delta (comment at 187-188 says 'For delta > 1 ... we assign the role once'), then sets quantity_granted to the full entitlement via the high-water-mark MAX() upsert (lines 96-104). The ticket role is a single binary 'has an emoji ticket' marker. Redemption (src/features/artistRotation/handlers.ts:125-145) removes that role after ONE use and tracks nothing about remaining quantity - there is no redeemed/remaining counter anywhere (confirmed: patreon_art_granted has only quantity_granted, migrations/062, and roles.ts:725-730 only displays it). After the first emoji redemption the role is gone, but getGrantedQuantity returns 2 >= entitlement 2, so the periodic sweep (patreonRoleDedup.ts:149-159 → handlePatreonArtRewards every 10 min) hits 'entitlement <= alreadyGranted' (line 180) and never re-grants. Net result: a Legendary Fiona patron pays for 2 emoji tickets but can only ever redeem 1. The economy under-pays the highest-tier supporter.

**Location:** `src/features/patreonArtRewards.ts:175-210`
**Category:** correctness | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** high (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Track redemptions, not just grants. Add a redeemed counter (or remaining = quantity_granted - redeemed) to patreon_art_granted; decrement/increment it in the /redeemreward confirm handler. Re-grant the ticket role whenever remaining > 0 and the user lacks it. Alternatively, model quantity>1 entitlements as multiple distinct redeemable units rather than one binary role. Until fixed, no tier should define quantity>1 for a single binary ticket role.

**Evidence:** full-codebase multi-agent audit 2026-05-31, finding A0263. See audit/2026-05-31/full-codebase-audit.md.
