# #00245 -- Periodic Patreon role-dedup sweep interval is never unref'd and has no stop function (only un-cleanable long-l

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** Low

sweepPatreonRoleStacks() creates a periodic 10-minute setInterval (line 178) but, unlike every other recurring timer in the bot process, it (a) does NOT call .unref() and (b) does not store the handle anywhere, so there is no way to clearInterval it. Cross-file impact: the entire shutdown contract in src/startup/ready.ts -> gracefulShutdown and src/startup/schedulers.ts -> stopSchedulers() assumes every recurring job is either unref'd or has a stopX() that is called during SIGTERM/SIGINT. Every scheduler (byteMultiplier, securityAudit, staleApplication, modMetrics, guildSnapshot, opsHealth, diskSpace, eventTimeout, badgeRefresh) and every feature timer (movieNight/gameNight persistence, dmVerification cleanup, modmail forwardedMessages cleanup, interactionCreate dedup, traceStore prune, notifyLimiter) follows that pattern. This one does not: there is no stopPatreonRoleDedup() anywhere in the source (verified by grep), and ready.ts never tears it down. Concrete impact is limited because the sweep callback is async and idle-cheap and gracefulShutdown ultimately calls process.exit(0), which terminates regardless. But the un-unref'd ref-counted timer means this module alone can keep Node's event loop alive (delaying a clean, signal-less exit), and it is uncancellable in tests/embeds (no opt-out, no handle), forcing test harnesses to either rely on process.exit or hang. It is the single defensible deviation from an otherwise airtight resource-cleanup convention.

**Location:** `src/features/patreonRoleDedup.ts:176-188`
**Category:** Resource leaks | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Match the established pattern: store the handle in a module-level let (e.g. let sweepInterval: NodeJS.Timeout | null), call sweepInterval.unref() right after creation, and export a stopPatreonRoleDedup() that clears it; then call that from the gracefulShutdown feature-cleanup section in src/startup/ready.ts alongside the other cleanups. Minimal fix if a stop hook is not wanted: append .unref() to the setInterval(...) call at line 178 (mirrors src/events/interactionCreate.ts:80 and src/features/modmail/routing.ts:144).

**Evidence:** full-codebase multi-agent audit 2026-05-31, finding A0302. See audit/2026-05-31/full-codebase-audit.md.
