# #00264 -- Perf pass T2: schedulers and background jobs

**Type:** refactor | **Priority:** Medium | **Status:** `WIP`

Third tier of the performance plan: cadence and cost of recurring work.

| Scheduler | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| patreonRoleDedup | 10 min, role.members filters + per-member fetch | 60 min, cache only |
| modMetrics | 15 min, 2 full action_log scans | 60 min, windowed |
| statsObservatory refresh | ~15 min, cohort N+1 and per-day WAU queries | 60 min, set-based |
| byteMultiplier | 60 s | 5 min |
| itemCapture | 10 s | keep; one audit-log fetch per guild per tick |

Also: audit runners iterate the member cache and batch writes in transactions; botDetection uses `LIMIT 1` instead of `COUNT(*)`; `/search` fallback queries `user_cache` before REST; movie/game night finalize wrapped in a transaction.

**Update 2026-09-01:** landed in seven commits (70df5d5f through 42148419). The observatory refresh is driven by `scripts/refresh-public-stats.ts`, not a bot scheduler, so its cadence is set wherever that script is cron'd; the two set-based queries landed. Left as is: byteMultiplier stays at 60 s (one indexed statement per minute; a 5 min lag on expiry was not worth it), the member audit still writes one auto-committed row per member (WAL with synchronous=NORMAL makes that cheap), and the NSFW audit does not yet skip unchanged avatar hashes.
