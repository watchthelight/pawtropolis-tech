# #00226 -- Modal-handler tests redefine patterns locally and never call the real handlers, providing no regression covera

**Type:** refactor | **Priority:** Low

Every block (`handleRejectModal`, `handleAcceptModal`, `handlePermRejectModal`, `handleKickModal`, `handleUnclaimModal`, plus the `action dispatching` block) asserts on locally-declared regexes (`MODAL_RE`, `ACCEPT_MODAL_RE`, `MODAL_PERM_REJECT_RE`, `MODAL_KICK_RE`, `MODAL_UNCLAIM_RE`) and inline string/`slice`/`trim` recomputations rather than importing and invoking the handlers in src/features/review/handlers/modals.ts. The local copies happen to match src/lib/modalPatterns.ts today (so no currently-false assertion), but if those production patterns or the handlers' parsing/dispatch (e.g. `reason = raw.trim().slice(0,500) || null`, the deferUpdate-then-resolveApplication-then-runXAction sequence) regress, none of these tests fail. The `action dispatching` cases like `expect("runRejectAction").toBe("runRejectAction")` are pure tautologies. Real risk surface (staff gate via requireInteractionStaff, deferUpdate timing, dispatch to the correct runner) is untested here.

**Location:** `tests/features/review/handlers/modals.test.ts:32-380`
**Category:** dead-code | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

Delete the string-equals-itself assertions.
