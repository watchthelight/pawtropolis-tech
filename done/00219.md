# #00219 -- getAvgClaimToDecision / getAvgSubmitToFirstClaim SQL is never actually exercised by any test

**Type:** refactor | **Priority:** Low

shared.test.ts only asserts these two functions are `typeof === 'function'` (lines 145-157) and explicitly documents (lines 142-144) that they are 'tested indirectly through the command handler tests that mock the shared module'. But stats/user.test.ts ALSO mocks them (user.test.ts lines 39-41 replace them with vi.fn()). The net result is that the real bodies of getAvgClaimToDecision/getAvgSubmitToFirstClaim in src/commands/stats/shared.ts (lines 60-132) - including their multi-CTE action_log JOIN SQL and the `c.claim_time < d.decision_time` / `c.claim_time > s.submit_time` guards - are executed by zero tests. A regression in that SQL (e.g. flipping the inequality, dropping a bind param, the INNER JOIN losing rows) would pass the entire suite green. This is a real coverage blind spot on the moderator-performance metric, not a wrong assertion.

**Location:** `tests/commands/stats/shared.test.ts:142-157`
**Category:** dead-code | **Confidence:** high | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Add a focused test that imports shared.ts WITHOUT mocking db (use an in-memory better-sqlite3 with a small action_log fixture, like tests/config.test.ts does) and asserts the computed averages for known claim/decision/submit timestamps, including the boundary where claim_time == decision_time (should be excluded).
