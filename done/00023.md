**Type:** chore | **Priority:** Medium | **Status:** `COMPLETE 2026-05-19`

**Resolution (2026-05-19):** Real count was ~1038 no-undef (worse than estimate). Expanded eslint.config.js (commit f2ffdc8):

- New block for `web/**/*.ts` with browser globals (document, window, navigator, HTML elements, DOM, etc.) plus Svelte 5 runes ($state, $derived, $effect, $props, $bindable, $inspect, $host).
- New block for `workers/**` with Cloudflare Workers runtime types.

Result: 1038 -> 95 no-undef warnings (-91%). Remaining 95 are mostly test-runner edge cases and rare APIs; not worth more time. Lint backlog dropped significantly toward zero.
