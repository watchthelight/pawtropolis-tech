# #00166 -- INSERT OR IGNORE keyed on whole-second joined_at_s silently drops a session on rapid same-second rejoin

**Type:** bug | **Priority:** Low

insertVoiceJoin inserts (guild_id, user_id, channel_id, joined_at_s) with INSERT OR IGNORE, and joined_at_s = floor(Date.now()/1000) (1-second resolution). The table's UNIQUE constraint is exactly (guild_id, user_id, channel_id, joined_at_s) (migrations/052_voice_session.ts:27). Scenario: a user with an open row for channel A at second T leaves (Case 2 closes that row, setting left_at_s=T) and rejoins channel A within the same second (Case 1). The new INSERT collides with the just-closed row's identical 4-tuple and is silently IGNORED, so no open session exists. The user's voice time in A is then untracked until a later second boundary. The same collision can happen on a quick A→B→A move sequence within one second. Voice minutes feed newsletter/pulse stats (web/src/lib/server/queries/pulse.ts:334-344), so this under-counts voice activity. Low frequency but real and silent.

**Location:** `src/features/voiceSessionTracker.ts:23-28, 57-59, 78-94`
**Category:** data-integrity | **Confidence:** medium | **Audit severity:** low (adversarially confirmed)

**Suggested fix:** Drop INSERT OR IGNORE in favor of a plain INSERT (the UNIQUE was meant only to dedupe true duplicates), or add a finer-grained discriminator to the uniqueness (e.g., include a monotonic counter or use millisecond precision for joined_at), or close+insert atomically while ensuring the new row cannot collide with the row just closed.

**Evidence:** full-codebase multi-agent audit 2026-05-31, finding A0114. See audit/2026-05-31/full-codebase-audit.md.
